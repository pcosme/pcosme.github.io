Pedro Cosme

The physics of flow, waves & chirality.

I study how collective motion gives rise to unusual transport and wave phenomena, from electrons flowing in graphene to waves on the surface of chiral fluids.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the group of Mazi Jalaal, University of Amsterdam. My work connects condensed matter, fluid dynamics, and nonlinear physics through theory and computation.

Background

My path began with plasma and condensed matter physics at Instituto Superior Técnico in Lisbon. Today I bring those tools to soft matter and chiral active fluids in Amsterdam.
